The ensemble icons.

From Mary Kate's "Ensemble Icons" folder, September 2026: one 250x250 pixel
sprite per person, in three flavours (an idle gif, a 360 turnaround gif, and a
still png). Only the 360 exists for all six, so that is the source here.

<slug>.gif  the turnaround, trimmed to its content box (the union across all
            frames, so the spin never jitters) and scaled to 160px tall with
            nearest-neighbour so the pixels stay crisp. Transparent background.
<slug>.png  frame 0 of the same, as the still.

Where they show: the line across the top of The Show: Live (gif, or png when
the visitor prefers reduced motion), the buddy list, the IM and profile
windows, and next to a byline on the blog, a playlist, or the wall whenever
the screen name is one of the cast. The slug lives in production_team.persona
as "avatar", so a new cast member needs a file here and that one word set.
